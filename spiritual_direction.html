Frequently Asked

Understanding spiritual direction

How is spiritual direction different from therapy or coaching?↓

Therapy often focuses on psychological wound healing and symptom relief. Coaching focuses on goal attainment. Spiritual direction is contemplative companionship: sitting together to listen for the deeper movements of your soul and the sacred in everyday life, without an agenda to fix or judge you.

Do I need to be religious or Christian?↓

No. Bread of Life is not a church. Our directors accompany people of all faith backgrounds — Catholic, Protestant, Jewish, Buddhist-informed, interspiritual — as well as those who are spiritual but not religious, agnostic, or in the midst of questioning religious dogma.

How often do sessions take place, and what are the fees?↓

Directees typically meet with their director once a month for 50–60 minutes. Directors operate on a sliding scale, typically $60 to $120 per session, and cost is never meant to be a barrier.
