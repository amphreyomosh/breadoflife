Our Mission

Community & Practice

“Bread of Life offers community and practices that support the intentional spiritual journey toward fullness of life and courageous offering of gifts to the world.”

  • Intentional Journey: Deep discernment, spiritual direction, and stillness.
  • Fullness of Life: Healing beyond burnout, fear, and scarcity.
  • Courageous Gifts: Releasing wisdom into our families and neighborhoods.

Methodology & Practice

What We Offer

Spiritual direction, cohorts, and contemplative gatherings. We use an adult learning model that provides context and facilitates reflection on a topic's relevance to one's own life, opening a window into what it means to be fully human and beloved.

01

Context & Grounding

Meeting seekers right where they are in daily life, honoring their particular vocational and family realities.

02

Facilitated Reflection

Quiet, structured spaces of contemplative inquiry to explore what lived experience is revealing.

03

Generative Action

Translating inner stillness into courageous, wholehearted service in the community and creation.

Sanctuary for Caregivers

Serving Those Who Serve

A dedicated heart for leaders and companions on the frontlines of community life.

Faith Communities

Clergy, chaplains & leaders

Confidential companioning and sabbatical space away from institutional demands.

Non-Profit Leaders

Advocates & directors

Refueling stamina and sustaining visionary hope through small cohort circles.

Healthcare Workers

Nurses, physicians & hospice

Safe processing of secondary trauma, grief, and moral injury in stillness.

Public & Social Services

Caseworkers & educators

Grounding public service in deep replenishment so giving comes from fullness.

Our Roots: Rooted & Open

A Foundation That Welcomes All

Our founders come from and practice in their own Christian traditions, a rooting which opens to and honors the weaving of the Spirit through diverse traditions. This foundation frees us to learn from, partner with, and serve all who come.

Our Fundamental Charter:

“The only expressions not welcome are those which diminish another, as we regard each person to have sacred worth and value.”
