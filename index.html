The inner life and the active life need each other.

Whether you are carrying questions you cannot put down, or doing work that has quietly worn you thin, Bread of Life is a place to slow down, listen, and find your footing again. Interfaith. Three decades in the Sacramento area. Open to anyone.

Whatever brought you here, you are not the first person to arrive with it.

If you are searching

Maybe you left a tradition, or never had one. Maybe you still find yourself asking what your life is for, and you have stopped expecting an institution to tell you. You are not looking for a belief system. You are looking for company, a little quiet, and practice that goes somewhere.

If you are already in the work

You teach, you care for someone, you organize, you show up. And somewhere along the way the thread between what you do and why you started doing it went thin. You do not need to escape the work. You need something underneath it that holds.

Either way, you have found the right place.

Starting with a Spiritual Director

There is no application to fill out and no test to pass. Just a few steps between where you are now and your first conversation.

Reach Out

Write to us using the address at the bottom of this page. Tell us as much or as little as you'd like about what brought you here.

Have a Conversation

We'll set up a short call to listen and help you find a director whose way of listening feels right for you.

Meet Together

You and your director begin meeting monthly, in person or by video, at a pace that stays yours to set.

Keep Returning

Spiritual direction isn't a program with an end date. Many people meet with their director for years.

Most people are matched with a director within two weeks of reaching out.
