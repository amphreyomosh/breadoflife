About the Lagerstrom Scholarship Fund

Ron and Velma Lagerstrom were beloved, longtime friends of Bread of Life. Ron served as a compassionate spiritual director at the Center, and Velma faithfully guided our community on the Board. When Ron passed in January 2021, this designated fund was established to honor his legacy of gentle, unconditional listening.

“This scholarship fund supports those on the margins who are in great need of support on the spiritual journey, but who, because of financial hardship, would otherwise not be able to participate.”

100% of designated gifts go directly to seeker scholarships

Whether you offer a one-time gift or a sustaining monthly pledge, your contribution keeps this interfaith contemplative community thriving so no seeker has to sit with life's real questions alone.
