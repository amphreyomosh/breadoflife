Meet Our Donors

Friends & Supporters

Individual Donors

We celebrate each person and couple whose faithful contributions, board guidance, and personal companionship foster deep roots for contemplative life across Sacramento, Davis, Auburn, and beyond.

Sustaining Donors

Julie & Pat Garvey

Longtime Supporters

Long-time friends and sustaining benefactors of Bread of Life. Julie and Pat’s ongoing commitment allows our programs, silent retreats, and community gatherings to flourish year after year.

Board & Leadership

Trudy Baltz & Chris Motley

Leadership & Companionship

Faithful companions within the contemplative circle. Chris Motley serves as Secretary of our Board of Directors, lending visionary governance alongside Trudy’s dedicated participation and heartfelt financial stewardship.

Founding Partners

Velma & Ron Lagerstrom

Scholarship Endowment Sponsors

Key supporters and founding partners who helped establish the Lagerstrom Scholarship Fund, ensuring that financial hardship never bars someone from receiving spiritual direction and accompaniment.

Foundational Benefactor

Albert Koyama

Lifetime & Estate Benefactor

Donated nearly $50,000 to the mission of Bread of Life during his lifetime. His niece Laura Sugano continued the family legacy with an estate gift honoring Albert’s sacred journey and deep wisdom.

Teacher & Mentor

Brother Don Bisson

Spiritual Director & Scholar

Internationally recognized spiritual director, Marist Brother, and Jungian scholar. Don has been an invaluable mentor, faculty contributor, and beloved partner to our directees and facilitators.

Remembrance

In Memoriam

Holding sacred memory of those who served as spiritual directors, board stewards, and community leaders, leaving behind an enduring lineage of wisdom and love.

“Over the years, the Bread of Life community has had the great blessing of working with some remarkable people. There are many who have served as spiritual directors, program faculty, or who have been active supporters and participants. These are a few of them that have left behind a legacy of love and friendship.” — Bread of Life Center Remembrance Archive

Rena Grant

June 12, 1945 – June 13, 2021
Spiritual Director (13 Years)

Rena was a spiritual director with Bread of Life for 13 years, driving monthly from the Bay Area with devotion to souls “at the edge.” Always ready to take the next step herself, she explored grief, aging, creative processes, and contemplative photography. She met her approaching death with acceptance, peace, and a gentle smile the day after her 76th birthday.

Honor with a Gift in Ways to Give →

Margo & Gill Hayes

Margo (1939 – 2020) • Gill (1935 – 2019)
The Margo & Gill Hayes Fund

Long-time friends and supporters of Bread of Life since our beginning. Margo served on the Board of Directors during our transition to an independent 501(c)(3) and co-shaped the Spirit in the Arts program. Several times a year, Gill would slide into the office with a wide smile to make a surprise donation honoring Margo. In 2018, they established the Margo and Gill Hayes Fund to support emerging community needs.

Donate to the Hayes Fund in Ways to Give →

Ron Lagerstrom

1932 – January 14, 2021
Spiritual Director (15 Years) • Scholarship Namesake

Ron served as a pastor for 50 years and joined Bread of Life as a spiritual director at age 67 for almost 15 years. An ongoing theme for him revolved around the inclusions of and contrasts between “beauty and brokenness.” He taught that spiritual direction is a sacred sanctuary where seekers are loved, affirmed, and met by God’s presence.

Support the Lagerstrom Scholarship Fund →

Albert Toshio Koyama

1919 – 2010
Foundational Benefactor

Born in Pasadena, California, to Japanese immigrants, Albert survived relocation to the Gila River internment camp during college. Finding deep meaning in life’s trials as “sacred story,” Albert became a foundational benefactor contributing nearly $50,000 during his lifetime, continued by his niece Laura Sugano’s estate gift.
