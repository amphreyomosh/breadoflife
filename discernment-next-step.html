Discernment When the Next Step Is Unclear

Practices for a decision you have been carrying a while.

7:00pm - 9:00pm
Format

Cost

Prerequisites
None
Open to anyone carrying a decision

Who this is for

Anyone carrying a decision that has stayed unresolved longer than expected. This event offers quiet space to notice what you know, what you fear, and what the next faithful step might be.

What happens

The session opens with a grounding practice, followed by guided reflection and quiet individual time. We will work with simple prompts for listening beneath urgency, then close with a practice for carrying one next step into the week.

  • 7:00 - Arrival and grounding practice
  • 7:20 - Guided reflection
  • 8:15 - Quiet discernment practice
  • 8:45 - Closing integration

What to bring

Something to write with and a quiet place where you can be undisturbed for two hours. Nothing else is required to participate.
