About Bread of Life

An interfaith contemplative community rooted in the Sacramento area for three decades — open to anyone carrying real questions.

Bread of Life began quietly, the way most lasting things do — with a handful of people who believed that spiritual life didn't have to mean choosing a side. Not everyone who walks through our door comes from the same tradition, or any tradition at all. What they share is a willingness to sit with the questions rather than rush past them.

We exist so that people carrying real questions do not have to sit with them alone.

Over the years, that willingness has taken different shapes: one-on-one spiritual direction, small gatherings, workshops built around a season of life rather than a doctrine. None of it asks you to arrive with the right beliefs. It asks you to arrive.

“The work isn't to convince anyone of anything. It's to make room.”

Listening

We practice the kind of attention that notices what is already moving beneath the surface of a life.

Hospitality

Everyone is invited to come as they are, without needing their beliefs or doubts to be fully polished.

Formation

We believe transformation is slower and deeper than performance; it grows through prayer, reflection, and trust.

Where we come from

Bread of Life grew out of a conviction shared by a small founding community: that people who have left institutional religion, or never found their way into one, still deserve companionship in the questions that matter most.

Sandra Lommasson, Founding Director

Sandra helped shape the earliest form of this community and continues to mentor directors today. Her full story lives in her director profile — this page is about what she helped build, not about her alone.

Where we are now

Today, Bread of Life supports a community of trained spiritual directors, hosts workshops throughout the year, and keeps building spaces — some quiet, some communal — for people to slow down and listen.

Come in as you are.

Whatever brought you here, you don't need the right words for it yet.
