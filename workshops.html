Frequently Asked

Workshop questions

Do I need any previous experience?

No. Our workshops are designed for beginners and experienced practitioners alike. Come as you are, with no preparation required.

Do I need to be religious or Christian?

No. Bread of Life welcomes people of every faith background, as well as people who are spiritual but not religious or still questioning.

Are workshops offered in person or online?

Both. Each listing includes the format and location so you can choose the setting that works best for you.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and anything that helps you settle in. We provide the prompts and guidance, and there is never required reading beforehand.

How much does a workshop cost?

Workshops are pay-what-you-can. Cost is never meant to be a barrier to attending, and you can reach out if you need help deciding what to contribute.

Not sure where to start?

A single workshop is the lowest-commitment door we have. Or reach out and we'll help you find your footing.
